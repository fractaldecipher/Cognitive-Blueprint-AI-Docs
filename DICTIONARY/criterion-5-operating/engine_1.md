### 🏛️ Vector Taxonomy Prefix Guide
All 24 vectors in this dictionary adhere to an integrated human-centered and somatic classification:

This domain outlines your ideal sensory regimen, nutritional assimilation style, and physical workspace compatibility. By curating what you consume physically, mentally, and environmentally, you safeguard your vitality and sustain peak cognitive clarity.

- `VEC_OM_BRAIN_...` / `COGNITIVE_...` — **Cognitive Processing Modes**: Navigating focused strategic thinking, adaptive peripheral absorption, and avoiding mental fog.
- `VEC_OM_DIET_...` / `DIGESTION_...` — **Sensory & Nutritional Intake**: Pacing meals, honoring digestive sensitivity, and optimizing nutritional assimilation.
- `VEC_OM_ENVIRONMENT_...` / `WORKSPACE_...` — **Terrain Compatibility & Workspace Harmony**: Selecting supportive physical environments, quiet workspaces, and natural lighting.
- `VEC_OM_INFORMATION_...` / `SENSORY_ABSORPTION_...` — **Information Ingestion & Filtering**: Curating data streams, avoiding sensory overload, and preserving mental clarity.

- **Vector Block Structure**:
  - **Phenomenon**: Core functional capability, contemplative stance, or environmental resonance.
  - **Mentor's Message**: Direct, compassionate lighthouse guidance to illuminate and elevate.
  - **Clinical Interpretation**: Compassionate, deep psychological insight illuminating natural tendencies and inner balance.
  - **Actionable Advice**: Practical, restorative guidance to cultivate clarity, peace, and natural vitality.
  - **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Compensatory conditioning, chronic stress patterns, or emotional and cognitive overextension.

### `VEC_OM_BRAIN_FOG_RISK_ACTIVE`
- **Phenomenon**: Digestion & Clarity: Sensitivity to Input Overload & Digestive Fatigue
- **Mentor's Message**: *"Brain fog is not a personal failing; it is your body's gentle signal that it needs rest from heavy inputs and uncurated noise."*
- **Clinical Interpretation**: Indicates an active sensitivity to mental clouding triggered by mistimed nutrition, heavy foods, or information overload. When dietary or sensory boundaries are strained, vitality diverts from mental clarity toward physical digestion, resulting in lethargy, slower discernment, and fatigue.
- **Actionable Advice**: Observe how your mind feels after meals and data intake. Practice light, nourishing eating during periods of important decision-making, and limit exposure to fast-paced news or social feeds. Keep yourself well-hydrated with pure water and gentle electrolytes.
- **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Consuming heavy meals or scrolling chaotic digital streams immediately prior to important creative sessions or deep-work periods.
