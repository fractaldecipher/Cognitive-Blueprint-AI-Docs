### 🏛️ Vector Taxonomy Prefix Guide
All 24 vectors in this dictionary adhere to an integrated human-centered and somatic classification:

This domain identifies your metabolic archetype, natural focus windows, and burnout mitigation pacing. By structuring your day around your natural metabolic rhythms rather than artificial schedules, you sustain vibrant physical vitality throughout life.

- `VEC_OM_BURNOUT_...` / `OVEREXTENSION_...` — **Burnout Mitigation & Pacing**: Recognizing exhaustion warning signs and preventing physical overextension.
- `VEC_OM_CONSTITUTION_...` / `STRUCTURAL_STABILITY_...` — **Constitutional Grounding & Posture**: Core constitutional stability, physical alignment, and metabolic grounding.
- `VEC_OM_DEEP_...` / `PEAK_...` — **Peak Focus Windows & Productivity**: Daily high-cognitive focus windows, deep work timing, and mental clarity peaks.
- `VEC_OM_ELEMENT_...` / `DIGESTIVE_...` — **Circadian Elemental Balance**: Aligning activity with daily elemental energy shifts (fire, water, earth, air, ether).
- `VEC_OM_EXERCISE_...` / `METABOLIC_...` — **Metabolic Archetype & Physical Movement**: Sprint versus sustained endurance profiles, restorative exercise, and metabolic pacing.

- **Vector Block Structure**:
  - **Phenomenon**: Core functional capability, contemplative stance, or environmental resonance.
  - **Mentor's Message**: Direct, compassionate lighthouse guidance to illuminate and elevate.
  - **Clinical Interpretation**: Compassionate, deep psychological insight illuminating natural tendencies and inner balance.
  - **Actionable Advice**: Practical, restorative guidance to cultivate clarity, peace, and natural vitality.
  - **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Compensatory conditioning, chronic stress patterns, or emotional and cognitive overextension.

### `VEC_OM_BURNOUT_RAPID_DEPLETION`
- **Phenomenon**: Energy Dynamics: Acute Adrenal Depletion & Sudden Exhaustion Risk
- **Mentor's Message**: *"When your inner reserves are empty, pushing forward causes sudden collapse. Honor your body's clear stop signs before fatigue forces an abrupt halt."*
- **Clinical Interpretation**: Indicates an innate vulnerability to sudden, sharp exhaustion rather than gradual fatigue. The individual tends to draw on adrenaline and mental willpower to sustain high output past natural physical limits. When reserves are finally exhausted, the body experiences a sudden shutdown that demands extended recovery.
- **Actionable Advice**: Establish firm daily boundaries based on bodily signals (eye strain, irritability, physical heaviness) rather than pushing until a project is completely finished. Protect daily rest as a non-negotiable health practice.
- **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Masking deep fatigue with excess caffeine or stimulants; ignoring the body's clear signals to pause and rest.

- **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Accepting chronic low-grade fatigue or disturbed sleep as normal; skipping planned restorative pauses simply because there is no immediate emergency.
