### 🏛️ Vector Taxonomy Prefix Guide
All 26 vectors in this dictionary adhere to an integrated human-centered and somatic classification:

This domain maps constitutional balance, dopamine reset windows, and zero-point decompression protocols. By creating intentional reset sanctuaries and honoring recovery thresholds, you restore neurochemical equilibrium and clear cellular fatigue.

- `VEC_OM_CELLULAR_...` / `CHRONIC_...` / `MITOCHONDRIAL_...` — **Cellular Exhaustion & Recovery**: Diagnosing deep tissue fatigue, mitochondrial recovery, and restoring baseline vitality.
- `VEC_OM_COMFORT_...` / `CONSTITUTION_...` — **Constitutional Comfort & Equilibrium**: Nourishing physical comfort zones, gentle movement, and somatic recalibration.
- `VEC_OM_DOPAMINE_...` / `EGO_...` — **Neurochemical & Ego Reset**: Dopamine fasting windows, releasing compulsive mental loops, and recalibrating reward systems.
- `VEC_OM_NEURAL_DECOMPRESSION_...` / `ZERO_...` — **Zero-Point Neural Decompression**: Entering deep parasympathetic stillness, nervous system recalibration, and sensory quietude.
- `VEC_OM_SENSORY_CUTOFF_...` / `SENSORY_OVERLOAD_...` — **Sensory Overload & Threshold Shields**: Establishing emergency sensory cutoffs and protecting against environmental saturation.

- **Vector Block Structure**:
  - **Phenomenon**: Core functional capability, contemplative stance, or environmental resonance.
  - **Mentor's Message**: Direct, compassionate lighthouse guidance to illuminate and elevate.
  - **Clinical Interpretation**: Compassionate, deep psychological insight illuminating natural tendencies and inner balance.
  - **Actionable Advice**: Practical, restorative guidance to cultivate clarity, peace, and natural vitality.
  - **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Compensatory conditioning, chronic stress patterns, or emotional and cognitive overextension.

### `VEC_OM_CELLULAR_BURNOUT_CRITICAL`
- **Phenomenon**: Cellular Care: Critical Fatigue & Mandatory Rest Period
- **Mentor's Message**: *"Your physical body has reached its absolute limit. Lay down all tools and responsibilities immediately, and allow life to restore you."*
- **Clinical Interpretation**: The physical body has crossed into a state of severe exhaustion. Vital energy reserves are empty, immune defense is compromised, and cellular repair processes are overwhelmed. Continuing to force work or intellectual effort at this stage risks serious physical illness, hormonal collapse, and emotional breakdown.
- **Actionable Advice**: Take an immediate, non-negotiable 2-to-4 week period of total rest. Step away from work responsibilities, turn off phones and computers, and focus completely on sleep, wholesome food, gentle outdoor walks, and medical rest.
- **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Attempting to negotiate "part-time" work while in a state of collapse; checking emails during what should be medical bedrest.

### `VEC_OM_CELLULAR_BURNOUT_HIGH`
- **Phenomenon**: Cellular Care: Elevated Physical Fatigue & Urgent Need to Slow Down
- **Mentor's Message**: *"Your body is sending clear signals of strain. Step back from the brink now before physical exhaustion forces you to stop."*
- **Clinical Interpretation**: Prolonged stress, sleep debt, and continuous work pressure are taking a measurable toll on physical well-being. Immunity is weakened, physical recovery is sluggish, and cognitive focus is fading. The body is calling for an intentional reduction in pace.
- **Actionable Advice**: Reduce your active workload and non-essential commitments by half immediately. Ensure 8 to 9 hours of peaceful sleep in a quiet room, avoid unnecessary travel, and nourish your body with wholesome, antioxidant-rich foods.
- **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Ignoring recurring physical signals (muscle stiffness, frequent colds, headaches, digestive upset) to chase arbitrary work deadlines.

### `VEC_OM_CELLULAR_REGENERATION_REQUIRED`
- **Phenomenon**: Cellular Care: Physical Replenishment & Targeted Nourishment
- **Mentor's Message**: *"Feed the roots so the tree can bloom again. Your physical body requires wholesome, natural nourishment to rebuild its strength."*
- **Clinical Interpretation**: Physical tissues and energy reserves need gentle, deep nourishment following an extended period of hard work. The body requires wholesome, nutrient-dense building blocks and peaceful rest to restore cellular health and vitality.
- **Actionable Advice**: Dedicate the next month to physical renewal: enjoy fresh, mineral-rich soups, healthy fats, clean proteins, fresh water, warm baths, and generous nighttime sleep.
- **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Eating ultra-processed convenience foods; skipping meals during busy days; relying on caffeine to push past bodily hunger and fatigue.

### `VEC_OM_CHRONIC_STRESS_PATTERN_ACTIVE`
- **Phenomenon**: Cellular Care: Lingering Stress Holding & Somatic Relaxation Need
- **Mentor's Message**: *"Soften your jaw, drop your shoulders, and breathe deeply. You do not need to carry yesterday's battles into this peaceful moment."*
- **Clinical Interpretation**: The nervous system is caught in a habit of chronic alertness. Stress hormones linger, leading to tight neck and shoulder muscles, shallow breathing, clenched teeth, and a feeling of being constantly on guard even in safe, quiet places.
- **Actionable Advice**: Practice daily somatic relaxation: gentle yoga, myofascial release or warm baths, slow and deep exhalations, and spending quiet time in peaceful natural settings.
- **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Engaging in high-stress, intense competition when your body is already holding chronic tension; ignoring tight muscles until they cause pain.

### `VEC_OM_COMFORT_CREATIVE_SANCTUARY`
- **Phenomenon**: Sanctuary Design: Dedicated Creative Space & Uninterrupted Haven
- **Mentor's Message**: *"Create a quiet space dedicated solely to your authentic craft. Let no worldly noise or administrative clutter breach this peaceful sanctuary."*
- **Clinical Interpretation**: The creative mind thrives when physical space is associated with peaceful focus. Mixing stressful administrative chores (bills, disputes, logistics) with deep writing, artistic creation, or philosophical thought in the same corner creates mental distraction. A dedicated creative space allows you to slip effortlessly into inspiration.
- **Actionable Advice**: Set aside a specific corner, desk, or private room exclusively for deep creative work and contemplation. Keep this area free of bills, administrative paperwork, and routine interruptions.
- **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Answering administrative emails or taking contentious phone calls while sitting in your dedicated creative sanctuary.

### `VEC_OM_COMFORT_RADICAL_PURGE`

- **Mentor's Message**: *"Clear the physical clutter from your surroundings. An orderly, simple room creates an open, peaceful mind."*
- **Clinical Interpretation**: Physical mess and visual chaos in your daily workspace place an ongoing subconscious demand on your attention. Piles of unread papers, loose items, and tangled cords create micro-distractions that gradually drain mental clarity.

- **Mentor's Message**: *"When the midday energy wanes, step into silence for a moment. A fifteen-minute quiet pause restores your focus for the afternoon."*

- **Actionable Advice**: Take a 15-to-20 minute quiet pause in the early afternoon: close your eyes in a dim room, listen to soft music, or take a peaceful stroll outdoors without your phone.
- **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Doubling your coffee intake in the afternoon; scrolling social media during your lunch break; scheduling difficult negotiations during your afternoon dip.

### `VEC_OM_NEURAL_DECOMPRESSION_MANDATORY`
- **Phenomenon**: Deep Stillness: The Cooling Pause Before Major Decisions
- **Mentor's Message**: *"Never make life-changing commitments while your mind is racing. Take a quiet, unhurried breath before speaking your choice."*
- **Clinical Interpretation**: Clear judgment is impaired when decisions are made while emotionally excited, stressed, or mentally saturated. Taking an intentional pause before signing agreements or committing to major changes guarantees wisdom.
- **Actionable Advice**: Establish a mandatory 24-to-48 hour cooling-off period before signing major contracts or making significant life changes. Spend this time in a quiet, peaceful place to allow your heart to speak clearly.
- **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Signing agreements under artificial pressure; making major financial commitments late at night or when upset.

### `VEC_OM_SENSORY_CUTOFF_PROTOCOL_ACTIVE`
- **Phenomenon**: Restorative Rhythm: Sensory Rest When Overwhelmed
- **Mentor's Message**: *"When the noise of the world becomes too loud, step back into your quiet harbor. Pure sensory stillness is your faithful medicine."*
- **Clinical Interpretation**: The sensory threshold has been temporarily exceeded. The nervous system feels overwhelmed, showing up as irritability, brain fog, or feeling on edge. Continued exposure to loud noise or screens only increases distress.
- **Actionable Advice**: Take an immediate 45-minute sensory pause: lie down in a dark, quiet room, put on a soft eye mask, and simply breathe gently without any agenda or screens.
- **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Trying to power through sensory overload; turning to alcohol or sedatives to numb the noise instead of resting in quiet.

- **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Stacking busy events back-to-back without rest periods; assuming that because you feel calm in the moment, your body doesn't need sleep.
