# DICTIONARY: CRITERION 1 — IDENTITY & CORE ARCHITECTURE

## Inquiry: _"What is my authentic path of learning, self-mastery, and sharing wisdom with the world?"_

> **System Classification**: Criterion 1 (Identity & Core Orientation)  
> **Total Vector Surface**: 20 Vectors  
> **Organization**: Strictly Alphabetical (A–Z)

---

### 🏛️ Vector Taxonomy Prefix Guide

All 20 vectors in this dictionary adhere to an integrated human-centered classification:

- `VEC_ID_KNOWLEDGE_...` — **Knowledge & Wisdom Domain**: Innate fields of natural mastery, contemplative depth, and practical aptitude.
- `VEC_ID_LEARNING_...` — **Learning & Transmission Profile**: The organic path through which you assimilate life lessons, validate truth through lived experience, and guide others.
- **Vector Block Structure**:
  - **Phenomenon**: Core innate capability, learning style, or transmission archetype.
  - **Mentor's Message**: Direct, compassionate lighthouse guidance to illuminate and elevate.
  - **Clinical Interpretation**: Compassionate, deep psychological insight illuminating your natural developmental journey.
  - **Actionable Advice**: Practical guidance to cultivate authentic mastery and transmit your gifts with grace.
  - **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Compensatory conditioning, exhaustion loops, or self-doubt.
