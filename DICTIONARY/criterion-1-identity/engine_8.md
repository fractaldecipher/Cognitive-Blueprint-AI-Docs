# DICTIONARY: CRITERION 1 — IDENTITY & CORE ARCHITECTURE

## Inquiry: _"What higher archetypal ideals, subtle currents, and conscious aspirations guide my presence?"_

> **System Classification**: Criterion 1 (Identity & Core Orientation)  
> **Total Vector Surface**: 20 Vectors  
> **Organization**: Strictly Alphabetical (A–Z)

---

### 🏛️ Vector Taxonomy Prefix Guide

All 20 vectors in this dictionary adhere to an integrated human-centered and archetypal classification:

- `VEC_ID_STELLAR_...` — **Guiding Ideals & Archetypal Currents**: Enduring values, noble aspirations, intuitive radars, and higher ethical callings that illuminate one's life path.
- `VEC_ID_STELLAR_INTENSITY_RISK` / `VEC_ID_STELLAR_AUTONOMIC_LOAD_CAUTION` — **Somatic Balance & Vital Renewal**: Navigating intense emotional thresholds, preventing nervous overload, and cultivating restorative equilibrium.
- **Vector Block Structure**:
  - **Phenomenon**: Core Archetype.
  - **Mentor's Message**: Illuminating reflection and compassionate guidance.
  - **Clinical Interpretation**: Insight into natural cognitive strengths, intuitive faculties, and systemic role.
  - **Actionable Advice**: Practical guidance to embody sovereign stewardship and clarity.
  - **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Compensatory habits, ego blind spots, or somatic exhaustion.

### `VEC_ID_STELLAR_AUTONOMIC_LOAD_CAUTION`

- **Phenomenon**: Nervous System Saturation & Somatic Decompression Call  
- **Mentor's Message**: *"The physical body is the sacred vessel of the soul. When the nervous system is overwhelmed, step back into silence before the vessel fractures."*
- **Clinical Interpretation**: An active physiological boundary signal. Indicates that prolonged high sensory or cognitive throughput has pushed the autonomic nervous system into sympathetic overactivity, manifesting as muscle tightness, shallow breathing, sleep disruptions, and cognitive restlessness.
- **Actionable Advice**: Immediately transition from hyper-engagement to intentional somatic unwinding. Implement non-negotiable periods of quiet, physical contact with nature, mindful breathing, and digital disconnection until natural equilibrium returns.
- **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Overriding biological fatigue signals with stimulants or stubborn willpower. Pushing through acute nervous exhaustion inevitably results in protracted burnout and emotional instability.
