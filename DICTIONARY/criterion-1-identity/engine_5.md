# DICTIONARY: CRITERION 1 — IDENTITY & CORE ARCHITECTURE

## Inquiry: _"What is my unshakeable moral compass, my soul's core impulse, and what protects me from self-betrayal?"_

> **System Classification**: Criterion 1 (Identity & Core Orientation)  
> **Total Vector Surface**: 38 Vectors  
> **Organization**: Strictly Alphabetical (A–Z)  

---

### 🏛️ Vector Taxonomy Prefix Guide
All 38 vectors in this dictionary adhere to an integrated human-centered classification:
- `VEC_ID_INTEGRITY_...` — **Dharma Backbone & Moral Spine**: Inner fidelity to truth, ethical resilience against compromise, and honoring authentic self-worth.
- `VEC_ID_SOUL_INTENT_...` — **Core Soul Calling**: Essential life purpose, heartfelt mission, and natural trajectory unclouded by external conditioning.
- `VEC_ID_SOUL_BASELINE_...` — **Emotional Sanctuary & Inner Safety**: Deep emotional roots, instinctive somatic equilibrium, and authentic sanctuary.
- `VEC_ID_SOUL_INDICATOR_...` — **Life Mastery Curriculum**: The central spiritual lesson, moral virtue, and noble path of personal mastery.
- **Vector Block Structure**:
  - **Phenomenon**: Core moral virtue, inner calling, or sanctuary baseline.
  - **Mentor's Message**: Direct, compassionate lighthouse guidance to illuminate and elevate.
  - **Clinical Interpretation**: Compassionate, deep psychological insight illuminating your moral spine, emotional balance, and true path.
  - **Actionable Advice**: Practical, restorative guidance to live in unshakeable alignment with your soul's truth.
  - **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Self-betrayal, moral fatigue, reactive defensiveness, or sacrificing enduring values for temporary approval.
