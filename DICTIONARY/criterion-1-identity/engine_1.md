### 🏛️ Vector Taxonomy Prefix Guide

All vectors in this dictionary adhere to an integrated human-centered classification:

- `VEC_ID_...` — **Identity Domain**: Foundational energetic constitution, authentic decision-making, inner authority, and cognitive equilibrium.
- **Vector Block Structure**:
  - **Phenomenon**: Core functional capability or operational state.
  - **Mentor's Message**: Direct, compassionate lighthouse guidance to illuminate and elevate.
  - **Clinical Interpretation**: Objective, diagnostic explanation of the individual's energetic wiring.
  - **Actionable Advice**: Practical operational shift to maintain high-fidelity throughput.
  - **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Compensatory conditioning, energetic leaks, or cognitive distortions.
