### 🏛️ Vector Taxonomy Prefix Guide
All 56 vectors in this dictionary adhere to an integrated human-centered and archetypal classification:
- `VEC_ID_NUM_LIFE_PATH_...` / `VEC_ID_NUM_MASTER_...` — **Life Path Pilgrimage**: The central journey of growth, natural gifts, and lifelong vocation.
- `VEC_ID_NUM_EXPRESSION_MASTER_...` / `VEC_ID_NUM_SOUL_URGE_MASTER_...` / `VEC_ID_NUM_PERSONALITY_MASTER_...` — **Master Callings**: Higher vibrational lessons of visionary insight, monumental building, and selfless mentorship.
- `VEC_ID_MYTHIC_ARCHETYPE_...` / `VEC_ID_MYTHIC_SHADOW_CHALLENGE` — **Mythic Archetypes**: Universal patterns of the human soul, inner trials, and the journey toward wholeness.
- `VEC_ID_NAME_DOUBLE_MASTER_RESONANCE` / `VEC_ID_NAME_PHONETIC_FRICTION` — **Vocal Resonance & Social Presence**: How your spoken voice and personal presence land in the human community.
- `VEC_ID_REFLEX_...` — **Innate Spontaneous Impulse**: Instinctive, natural reactions and daily personal gifts.
- **Vector Block Structure**:
  - **Phenomenon**: Core archetypal expression, natural virtue, or life theme.
  - **Mentor's Message**: Direct, compassionate lighthouse guidance to illuminate and elevate.
  - **Clinical Interpretation**: Compassionate, deep psychological insight illuminating your soul's journey and inner potential.
  - **Actionable Advice**: Practical, uplifting guidance to walk your path with dignity, joy, and peace.
  - **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Compensatory conditioning, exhaustion, ego defensiveness, and self-doubt.
