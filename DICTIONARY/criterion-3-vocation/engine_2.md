### 🏛️ Vector Taxonomy Prefix Guide
All 41 vectors in this dictionary adhere to an integrated human-centered and vocational classification:

This domain decodes your strategic market alignment, economic leverage points, and sector efficiency. By identifying the domains where your cognitive gifts meet genuine market demand, you eliminate dissipated effort and focus your kinetic energy toward high-yield, sustainable contribution.

- `VEC_VOC_CAREER_EFFICIENCY_...` — **Operational Efficiency & Focus**: Evaluating whether energy translates directly into impact or is dissipated across fragmented fronts.
- `VEC_VOC_FINANCIAL_AXIS_...` — **Financial Architecture**: Balancing direct personal mastery with leveraged partnerships and equity stewardship.
- `VEC_VOC_MACRO_SECTOR_...` — **Macro Economic Domains**: Identifying primary industry sectors (analytical precision, executive governance, visionary leadership, kinetic defense, contrarian innovation, diplomatic prosperity, social resonance, systemic compliance, or turnaround consolidation).
- `VEC_VOC_MICRO_SUBSECTOR_...` — **Niche Specialization**: Carving out uncontested micro-niche dominance through precision positioning.
- `VEC_VOC_PRECISION_DRIVER_...` — **Core Execution Drivers**: The specific cognitive engines that drive your commercial momentum and market breakthroughs.
- `VEC_VOC_PRECISION_SENTINEL_...` — **Quality Gatekeepers & Risk Shields**: Internal standards safeguarding the enterprise from sloppy data, reputational dilution, reckless expansion, or legal vulnerabilities.
- `VEC_VOC_SECTOR_POWER_...` — **Sector Concentration & Portfolio Health**: Managing focused specialization versus scattered multi-industry dilution.
- `VEC_VOC_ZENITH_...` — **Apex Public Authority & Reputation**: Embodying public thought leadership, institutional status, and enduring legacy building.

- **Vector Block Structure**:
  - **Phenomenon**: Core functional capability, contemplative stance, or environmental resonance.
  - **Mentor's Message**: Direct, compassionate lighthouse guidance to illuminate and elevate.
  - **Clinical Interpretation**: Compassionate, deep psychological insight illuminating natural tendencies and inner balance.
  - **Actionable Advice**: Practical, restorative guidance to cultivate clarity, peace, and natural vitality.
  - **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Compensatory conditioning, chronic stress patterns, or emotional and cognitive overextension.
