### 🏛️ Vector Taxonomy Prefix Guide
All 20 vectors in this dictionary adhere to an integrated human-centered and vocational classification:

This domain articulates your authentic executive command structure, decision-making style, and institutional authority. By understanding your natural leadership archetype, you guide teams, projects, and organizations with integrity, clarity, and enduring influence.

- `VEC_VOC_COGNITIVE_STYLE_...` — **Strategic Cognitive Leadership**: Executive decision style, visionary synthesis, analytical rigor, and relational alliance architecture.
- `VEC_VOC_COMMAND_CHAIN_...` — **Command Architecture & Governance**: Autonomous apex command, dual co-leadership, or decentralized collaborative governance.
- `VEC_VOC_DRIVER_...` — **Core Leadership Drivers**: Innate archetypal motivations steering executive action, cultural authority, and institutional stewardship.
- `VEC_VOC_MARKET_ROLE_...` — **Primary Market Archetypes**: Operating as a Systems Architect, Kinetic Builder, Strategic Initiator, or Trusted Evaluator.
- `VEC_VOC_PLANETARY_...` — **Archetypal Executive Postures**: Grounding authority in visionary expansion or disciplined structural stability.

- **Vector Block Structure**:
  - **Phenomenon**: Core functional capability, contemplative stance, or environmental resonance.
  - **Mentor's Message**: Direct, compassionate lighthouse guidance to illuminate and elevate.
  - **Clinical Interpretation**: Compassionate, deep psychological insight illuminating natural tendencies and inner balance.
  - **Actionable Advice**: Practical, restorative guidance to cultivate clarity, peace, and natural vitality.
  - **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Compensatory conditioning, chronic stress patterns, or emotional and cognitive overextension.
