### 🏛️ Vector Taxonomy Prefix Guide
All 14 vectors in this dictionary adhere to an integrated human-centered and vocational classification:

This domain maps your capital foundation, asset accumulation preferences, and cashflow dynamics. By establishing balance between solar expansion and lunar preservation, you build an enduring financial reservoir that sustains your purpose across generations.

- `VEC_VOC_ASSET_...` — **Asset Foundation Pillars**: Core asset repositories including intellectual property, equity partnerships, and sovereign holdings.
- `VEC_VOC_CAPITAL_LUNAR_...` / `CAPITAL_SOLAR_...` — **Capital Polarity Dynamics**: Balancing solar wealth expansion and outward accumulation with lunar capital preservation and security.
- `VEC_VOC_CAPITAL_RESERVOIR_...` — **Capital Reservoir Health**: Managing liquid reserves, asset stability, and sustainable financial foundations.
- `VEC_VOC_CASHFLOW_...` — **Cashflow Mechanics & Circulation**: Optimizing income velocity, recurring cash generation, and financial pipeline flow.
- `VEC_VOC_WEALTH_STAGNATION_...` / `WEALTH_VELOCITY_...` — **Wealth Velocity & Stagnation**: Diagnosing capital bottlenecks and accelerating healthy economic circulation.

- **Vector Block Structure**:
  - **Phenomenon**: Core functional capability, contemplative stance, or environmental resonance.
  - **Mentor's Message**: Direct, compassionate lighthouse guidance to illuminate and elevate.
  - **Clinical Interpretation**: Compassionate, deep psychological insight illuminating natural tendencies and inner balance.
  - **Actionable Advice**: Practical, restorative guidance to cultivate clarity, peace, and natural vitality.
  - **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Compensatory conditioning, chronic stress patterns, or emotional and cognitive overextension.

### `VEC_VOC_ASSET_PILLAR_INTELLECTUAL_PROPERTY`
- **Phenomenon**: Asset Foundation: Intellectual Property, Creative Works & Proprietary Knowledge
- **Mentor's Message**: *"Your intellectual insights and creative creations are your highest-yielding foundations. Build durable catalogs of proprietary knowledge that provide lasting security."*
- **Clinical Interpretation**: Identifies strong asset foundation alignment with intellectual property, proprietary frameworks, educational curricula, authored publications, media archives, and licensing rights. The individual generates maximum financial leverage by converting authentic creative breakthroughs into formally protected, durable assets. Somatically, it relieves chronic physical strain by supplementing direct labor with recurring royalties and scalable distribution of ideas.
- **Actionable Advice**: Secure, copyright, and formally protect your core methodologies, writings, and educational architectures. Structure licensing agreements that yield recurring value while allowing you creative freedom.
- **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Distributing valuable proprietary frameworks without clear attribution or licensing protections; remaining locked in hourly labor when foundational knowledge assets could be shared and scaled.
