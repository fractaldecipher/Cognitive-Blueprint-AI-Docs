### 🏛️ Vector Taxonomy Prefix Guide
All 26 vectors in this dictionary adhere to an integrated human-centered and vocational classification:

This domain maps your underlying cognitive engines, natural energy rhythms, and primary talent clusters. By understanding whether you operate on sustained stamina, precision bursts, emotional waves, or analytical clarity, you organize your professional life to work in harmony with your natural design.

- `VEC_VOC_BUSINESS_SKILL_...` — **Core Strategic Capabilities**: Market magnetism, capital stewardship, long-term strategic vectoring, and objective systems auditing.
- `VEC_VOC_CIRCUITRY_...` / `FREQUENCY_BAND_...` — **Operational Frequencies**: Engaging collective empirical scaling, solitary mutative breakthroughs, or high-trust communal stewardship.
- `VEC_VOC_MOTOR_...` — **Energetic Drivers & Endurance**: Navigating sustained generative stamina, willpower commitments, adrenaline momentum, and emotional wave clarity.
- `VEC_VOC_TALENT_CLUSTER_...` — **Primary Talent Clusters**: Deploying analytical precision, creative expression, kinetic execution, relational mastery, or structural engineering.

- **Vector Block Structure**:
  - **Phenomenon**: Core functional capability, contemplative stance, or environmental resonance.
  - **Mentor's Message**: Direct, compassionate lighthouse guidance to illuminate and elevate.
  - **Clinical Interpretation**: Compassionate, deep psychological insight illuminating natural tendencies and inner balance.
  - **Actionable Advice**: Practical, restorative guidance to cultivate clarity, peace, and natural vitality.
  - **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Compensatory conditioning, chronic stress patterns, or emotional and cognitive overextension.
