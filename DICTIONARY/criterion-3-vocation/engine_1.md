### 🏛️ Vector Taxonomy Prefix Guide
All 43 vectors in this dictionary adhere to an integrated human-centered and vocational classification:

This domain articulates the foundational architecture of your life's work, primary operational role, and career maturation. By illuminating your natural working style, energetic rhythm, and skill reservoir, you transition from reactive labor into purposeful craftsmanship and grounded commercial authority.

- `VEC_VOC_CORE_MISSION_...` — **Vocational Mission State**: Conscious alignment with your primary operational purpose versus dormant incubation.
- `VEC_VOC_CORE_ROLE_...` — **Primary Market Archetypes**: Operating as a Systems Advisor, Kinetic Builder, Objective Evaluator, or Strategic Initiator.
- `VEC_VOC_EXECUTION_...` / `KINETIC_...` — **Energetic Capacity & Throughput**: Balancing sustained execution with precision burst delivery.
- `VEC_VOC_FREQUENCY_...` / `OPERATIONAL_MODALITY_...` — **Operating Modalities**: Engaging disruptive innovation, trust-based alliances, or scalable systemic validation.
- `VEC_VOC_FRICTION_...` — **Alchemical Transmutation of Career Blocks**: Transforming professional vulnerability and friction into your highest commercial expertise.
- `VEC_VOC_LIFECYCLE_...` — **Career Maturation & Trajectory**: Navigating apprenticeship phases, threshold milestones, and personal versus collaborative destinies.
- `VEC_VOC_MASTERY_RULER_...` — **Mastery Archetypes & Enduring Principles**: Grounding leadership in solar vision, mercurial intellect, martian courage, venetian harmony, jupiterian expansion, lunar empathy, or saturnian endurance.
- `VEC_VOC_NODE_TRAJECTORY_...` — **Evolutionary Career Trajectory**: Bridging foundational competencies with frontier growth.
- `VEC_VOC_SKILL_...` — **Skill Reservoir & Commercialization**: Deepening multi-domain expertise and packaging technical mastery into tangible market offerings.

- **Vector Block Structure**:
  - **Phenomenon**: Core functional capability, contemplative stance, or environmental resonance.
  - **Mentor's Message**: Direct, compassionate lighthouse guidance to illuminate and elevate.
  - **Clinical Interpretation**: Compassionate, deep psychological insight illuminating natural tendencies and inner balance.
  - **Actionable Advice**: Practical, restorative guidance to cultivate clarity, peace, and natural vitality.
  - **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Compensatory conditioning, chronic stress patterns, or emotional and cognitive overextension.

- **Mentor's Message**: *"True mastery begins with humble, patient repetition. Pick your core craft, honor the basics, and dig deep roots."*
- **Clinical Interpretation**: Indicates an exploratory or early stage of skill acquisition. While natural talent is strong, technical mastery across complex scenarios requires sustained, deliberate practice and hands-on experience.
