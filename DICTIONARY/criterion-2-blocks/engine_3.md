### 🏛️ Vector Taxonomy Prefix Guide
All 27 vectors in this dictionary adhere to an integrated human-centered and somatic classification:

This domain decodes deep existential obligations, recurring life lessons, and developmental thresholds that guide personal maturation. By illuminating these patterns, you move beyond unconscious repetition, learning to meet life's resistance with patience, dismantle outdated survival vows, and step into grounded, ethical sovereignty.

- `VEC_BLK_EVOLUTIONARY_MATURITY_...` — **Developmental Maturation Stages**: Foundational grounding, transformative transitions, and sovereign ethical stewardship.
- `VEC_BLK_GRAVITY_...` — **Environmental Tides & Resistance**: Navigating headwind periods of character refinement and tailwind periods of unforced momentum.
- `VEC_BLK_KARMIC_BETRAYAL_...` / `LABOR_...` / `POWER_...` / `TOWER_...` — **Core Developmental Crucibles**: Life lessons centered on sensory temperance, patient craftsmanship, ethical power, and the dismantling of pride.
- `VEC_BLK_KARMIC_PROTOCOL_...` — **Conscious Remediation Protocols**: Practical inner disciplines including disciplined labor, ego humility, sensory recalibration, and generous service.
- `VEC_BLK_KARMIC_ECLIPSE_...` / `SAROS_...` — **Epochal Crossroads & Rhythms**: Significant life milestones, threshold transitions, and long-term chapters of growth.
- `VEC_BLK_NODAL_...` — **Evolutionary Vectors & Balances**: Synthesizing hard-won wisdom from the past with the courageous exploration of future growth.
- `VEC_BLK_SAMSARA_...` / `SKIPPED_STEP_...` — **Resolving Repetitive Loops**: Deconditioning recursive relationship scripts and mastering overlooked developmental lessons.
- `VEC_BLK_VOW_...` — **Subconscious Vows & Self-Imposed Limits**: Dismantling archaic vows of financial deprivation or social invisibility.

- **Vector Block Structure**:
  - **Phenomenon**: Core functional capability, contemplative stance, or environmental resonance.
  - **Mentor's Message**: Direct, compassionate lighthouse guidance to illuminate and elevate.
  - **Clinical Interpretation**: Compassionate, deep psychological insight illuminating natural tendencies and inner balance.
  - **Actionable Advice**: Practical, restorative guidance to cultivate clarity, peace, and natural vitality.
  - **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Compensatory conditioning, chronic stress patterns, or emotional and cognitive overextension.
