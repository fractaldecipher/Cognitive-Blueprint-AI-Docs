### `VEC_BLK_ADVERSARY_LITIGATION_EXPOSURE`

- **Phenomenon**: Contractual Ambiguity & Predatory Exposure
- **Mentor's Message**: *"Fortify your perimeter before the storm arrives. An ambiguous agreement is an open invitation to misunderstanding and exploitation."*
- **Clinical Interpretation**: Activates vulnerabilities arising from informal agreements, loose contractual language, or naive trust in business or legal arrangements. Lacking formal protective documentation, the individual becomes exposed to bad-faith actors and contentious disputes, which triggers chronic hyper-vigilance and somatic anxiety.
- **Actionable Advice**: Subject all partnerships, agreements, and key commitments to rigorous legal review. Eliminate ambiguous clauses, specify clear deliverables, and require written confirmation for every strategic mandate.
- **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Relying on informal handshake deals, assuming goodwill replaces legal clarity, ignoring contractual loopholes, or delaying professional counsel out of conflict avoidance.
