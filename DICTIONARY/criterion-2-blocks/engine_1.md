# DICTIONARY: CRITERION 2 — BLOCKS, DEFENSES & INTEGRATION ARCHITECTURE

## Inquiry: _"What reactive defense patterns, unconscious inhibitors, and deep emotional patterns drain my vital energy, and how can they be transformed into wisdom?"_

> **System Classification**: Criterion 2 (Blocks & Defensive Patterns)  
> **Total Vector Surface**: 124 Vectors  
> **Organization**: Strictly Alphabetical (A–Z)

---

### 🏛️ Vector Taxonomy Prefix Guide

All 124 vectors in this dictionary adhere to an integrated human-centered and somatic classification:

- `VEC_BLK_CATALYST_...` — **Transmutation Catalysts**: Turning existential wounds and hardships into mature wisdom, authentic boundaries, and deep service.
- `VEC_BLK_CORE_WOUND_...` — **Core Emotional Wounds**: Foundational vulnerabilities and fears (abandonment, betrayal, vulnerability, powerlessness) seeking compassionate healing.
- `VEC_BLK_ENTROPY_...` — **Entropy & Energy Leaks**: Cognitive fatigue, dispersal of focus, cynicism, finish-line anxiety, and vital depletion.
- `VEC_BLK_INHIBITOR_SPHERE_...` — **Subconscious Inhibitors**: Emotional and behavioral defense habits (rigidity, rage, discord, lethargy, detachment, avoidance).
- `VEC_BLK_PATTERN_CLUSTER_...` — **Synergistic Pattern Clusters**: Clustered emotional themes and creative currents shaping human experience.
- `VEC_BLK_REACTIVITY_NODE_...` — **Reactive Reflexes & Thresholds**: 64 instinctual stress responses and defensive habits, showing how each contraction holds the seed of an authentic gift.
- `VEC_BLK_SHELL_...` — **Protective Armor & Behavioral Shells**: 12 characteristic perceptual filters and defensive masks adopted when feeling unsafe.

- **Vector Block Structure**:
  - **Phenomenon**: Core functional challenge, emotional tension, or archetypal pattern.
  - **Mentor's Message**: Compassionate lighthouse beacon and guiding perspective.
  - **Clinical Interpretation**: Diagnostic insight into underlying somatic patterns, defensive loops, and energy drains.
  - **Actionable Advice**: Practical behavioral shifts, mindful boundaries, and somatic grounding practices.
  - **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Compensatory traps, self-sabotaging cycles, and cognitive distortions.

- **Mentor's Message**: *"The joy of living is not found in an imaginary world where everything is perfected, but in the vitality that improves life with love."*
- **Clinical Interpretation**: A relentless, nagging dissatisfaction that instantly detects the imperfection in every joy, achievement, or companion.
