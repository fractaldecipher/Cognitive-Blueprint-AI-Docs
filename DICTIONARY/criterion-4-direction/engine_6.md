All 19 vectors in this dictionary adhere to an integrated human-centered and strategic classification:

This domain measures your systemic escape velocity, multi-generational wealth preservation potential, and permanent resolution of ancestral limitations. By constructing an unshakeable sovereign architecture, you establish enduring freedom that blesses future generations.

- `VEC_DIR_ANCESTRAL_...` / `KNOT_...` — **Ancestral Resolution & Karmic Knots**: Dissolving generational trauma knots, scarcity conditioning, and historical financial ceilings.
- `VEC_DIR_ESCAPE_...` / `SYSTEMIC_...` — **Systemic Escape Velocity**: Achieving authentic systemic independence, jurisdictional flexibility, and operational freedom.
- `VEC_DIR_GENERATIONAL_...` / `FINANCIAL_...` — **Generational Wealth Preservation**: Multi-generational wealth trusts, values transmission, and protecting capital across decades.
- `VEC_DIR_IP_...` / `LEGACY_...` — **Enduring Legacy & IP Perpetuity**: Codifying intellectual property, publishing enduring works, and establishing institutional foundations.
- `VEC_DIR_SOVEREIGN_WEALTH_...` — **Sovereign Capital Architecture**: Establishing self-sustaining endowment foundations, unassailable balance sheets, and philanthropic vehicles.

- **Vector Block Structure**:
  - **Phenomenon**: Core functional capability, contemplative stance, or environmental resonance.
  - **Mentor's Message**: Direct, compassionate lighthouse guidance to illuminate and elevate.
  - **Clinical Interpretation**: Compassionate, deep psychological insight illuminating natural tendencies and inner balance.
  - **Actionable Advice**: Practical, restorative guidance to cultivate clarity, peace, and natural vitality.
  - **System Friction (To Avoid)**: Compensatory conditioning, chronic stress patterns, or emotional and cognitive overextension.
