### `SOVEREIGNTY_RISK` — Intellectual Property & Scale Sovereignty Risk
* **System Tension / Mechanism**: A critical structural friction where commercial expansion velocity outpaces sovereign legal, technical, and governance shielding. As the enterprise scales, the operator delegates core IP, software code, client access, or treasury control without establishing ironclad contracts, equity lockups, or cryptographic security protocols. This creates an extreme vulnerability to hostile takeovers, IP theft, co-founder mutinies, or corporate extortion at the exact moment the company reaches peak market valuation.
* **Mentor's Diagnosis**: You are building a skyscraper on a sand foundation. In your rush for rapid market capture, you have left your crown jewels unlocked. Scaling an unprotected asset does not make you rich; it makes you a prime target for corporate predators and disgruntled partners.
* **Strategic Remediation / Actionable Guidance**:
  1. *Immediate IP Lockup*: Formalize all ownership of trademarks, patents, proprietary code, and operational frameworks inside a dedicated, isolated Holding Entity.
  2. *Vesting & Non-Compete Scaffolding*: Ensure all co-founders, executives, and contractors sign strict IP assignment, vesting schedules, and non-compete agreements before granting further access.
  3. *Treasury Compartmentalization*: Separate daily operational bank accounts from core capital vaults. Require dual-authorization on all capital transfers above threshold limits.
