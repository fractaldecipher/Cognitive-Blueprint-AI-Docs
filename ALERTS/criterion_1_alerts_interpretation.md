### `SPLIT_DEFINITION_BRIDGE_TENSION` — Split Energetic Definition Bridge Tension
* **System Tension / Mechanism**: The internal architecture features two or more isolated functional islands (Split Definition) within the BodyGraph or elemental matrix. These processing centers operate independently without native, internal channels connecting them. Consequently, the operator experiences an inherent cognitive/somatic gap—a structural friction where decisions made in one definition island cannot communicate smoothly with the other without an external bridging frequency (gate, planet, or relational aura). This creates a recurring vulnerability where the operator compulsively seeks external people or environments to "feel whole," risking energetic conditioning and strategic dependence.
* **Mentor's Diagnosis**: Your inner constitution is not broken; your architecture is designed with distinct, specialized chambers of consciousness. The tension you feel is the natural polarity between isolated centers of awareness striving to connect. The error occurs when the analytical mind interprets this gap as a personal deficiency and invites toxic bridging relationships simply to feel complete.
* **Strategic Remediation / Actionable Guidance**:
  1. *Bridge Identification*: Audit the exact gate or planetary frequency that bridges your definition. Recognize this as a functional interface, not an emotional need.
  2. *Relational Quarantine*: Never enter partnerships solely because someone "completes" your definition. Use your sovereign decision authority (Sacral/Emotional/Splenic) to vet the bridge.
  3. *Environmental Bridging*: Utilize ambient transit activations or specialized work environments (e.g., Markets or Public Hubs) to bridge definitions passively without incurring relational debt.

### `AUTONOMOUS_COGNITIVE_CORE` — Fully Integrated Autonomous Cognitive Core
* **System Tension / Mechanism**: The internal architecture possesses a Single Definition or a fully consolidated Almuten/Atmakaraka sovereign core. The bio-cognitive faculties are seamlessly integrated, forming a self-contained, unified axis of awareness. While this produces extraordinary internal speed, independence, and immunity to external conditioning, it creates a systemic blindspot: high resistance to external input and a tendency to overlook critical environmental feedback. The operator risks operating in an insulated vacuum, executing strategies without adapting to shifting external realities.
* **Mentor's Diagnosis**: You possess an extraordinary, self-sustaining inner core. Your independence is your greatest asset, but your isolation is your primary vulnerability. Because you do not naturally rely on others to reach clarity, you risk dismissing external counsel as noise rather than vital perspective.
* **Strategic Remediation / Actionable Guidance**:
  1. *Structured Audit Protocols*: Implement mandatory 360-degree feedback loops and external data audits before finalizing major strategic deployments.
  2. *Deliberate Delay*: Even with single definition speed, enforce a 24-hour cooling buffer on irreversible capital and organizational commitments.
  3. *Selective Receptivity*: Appoint dedicated "Evaluators" or "Advisors" whose sole role is to challenge your internal consensus with hard empirical facts.

### `MIND_AUTHORITY_DECOUPLING` — Mind Authority Decoupling & Not-Self Hijack
* **System Tension / Mechanism**: A severe cross-engine clash between the conceptual Ajna/Head processing center (the Mind) and the somatic Decision Authority (Sacral Gut, Emotional Wave, Splenic Instinct, or Ego). The logical mind attempts to usurp the executive throne, formulating strategies, timelines, and commitments based on external pressures, anxiety, or intellectual rationalization. This creates immediate somatic friction: adrenal fatigue, stomach tightness, emotional turbulence, and strategic misfires caused by acting without somatic clearance.
* **Mentor's Diagnosis**: The mind is a brilliant research laboratory and a terrible chief executive officer. When your mind attempts to make life decisions, it operates on fear and conditioning. Every time you override your body's visceral "Yes/No" with intellectual logic, you trigger bio-chemical inflammation and execution drag.
* **Strategic Remediation / Actionable Guidance**:
  1. *Demote the Mind*: Use the mind exclusively for research, modeling, measurement, and communication—never for deciding *what* to do or *when* to act.
  2. *Somatic Gatekeeping*: Before signing any contract or initiating any project, perform a mandatory Somatic Check. If the Sacral/Splenic/Emotional authority does not approve, the answer is an absolute "No," regardless of how logical the deal appears.
  3. *Deconditioning Fasting*: Practice 48-hour periods of "No-Decision Isolation" to quiet mental chatter and allow true somatic authority to re-emerge.

### `EMPIRICAL_INTUITIVE_BRIDGE` — Empirical-Intuitive Dual Channel Bridge
* **System Tension / Mechanism**: A high-voltage convergence between the Epimethean/Mercury analytical processing channels (empirical data, logic, historical audits) and the Promethean/Draconic intuitive channels (direct gnosis, master pattern recognition). When these two systems fire simultaneously, the operator experiences cognitive oscillation: the rational mind demands empirical data and spreadsheets, while the somatic intuition already knows the outcome in advance. If unintegrated, this produces analysis paralysis or second-guessing of clear intuitive flashes.
* **Mentor's Diagnosis**: You are equipped with both radar (intuition) and optics (empirical analysis). The tension arises because radar detects targets before optics can see them. Trying to force your intuition to "prove itself" algebraically before acting destroys your competitive speed advantage.
* **Strategic Remediation / Actionable Guidance**:
  1. *Sequential Processing Protocol*: Allow intuition to set the direction and identify opportunities; use empirical analysis to verify the structural integrity and execution logistics.
  2. *Intuative Ledger*: Maintain a private record of unverified intuitive flashes and track their accuracy against subsequent data to build conscious trust in your non-linear perception.
  3. *Synthesis Rule*: Never ignore a strong intuitive warning simply because the current spreadsheet looks favorable. Investigate deeper until the hidden flaw is revealed.
